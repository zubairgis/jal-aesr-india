India · agro-ecological context and pilot water regimesUse the layer control, click an AESR, or select one of the three proof-of-need landscapes.

Groundwater buffering

Groundwater access can sustain irrigation when rainfall is deficient, while transferring pressure to aquifer storage. Agricultural supply can therefore appear resilient even as subsurface stress accumulates.

Rainfall dependence

Where irrigation buffering is limited, crop-water exposure responds more directly to monsoon timing, dry spells and intra-seasonal rainfall variability.

Surface-water buffering

Reservoirs, rivers, tanks and canals store or route water across space and time, so agricultural water availability may differ substantially from immediate local rainfall.

Pilot evidence → national scaling

The three locations illustrate mechanisms, not national representativeness. The proposed Research Chair will extend the framework across India’s agro-ecological regions using monitored groundwater and surface water, rainfall, crop timing, irrigation dependence, NISAR, IRS and complementary Earth observation.
